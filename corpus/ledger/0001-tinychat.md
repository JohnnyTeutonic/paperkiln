# Ledger 0001 — TinyChat (v1, v2)

*Covers `tinychat_v1_1500`, `tinychat_v2_1500`, `tinychat_v2_4000`. Written
16 Oct 2026 from `tools/get_tinychat_data.py`, `tools/tinychat_v2.py`,
`transformer_cpp/CHAT_EXPERIMENTS.md` and commits `3a489c5` and `b579b0e`
(6 Oct 2026).*

**1. Task.** Single-exchange small talk: `user: … assistant: …`, one
dialogue per line, ending in `<|endoftext|>`.

**2. Target capability.** A model that answers the question asked, inside a
closed world of eight topics (how are you, food, drinks, outings, weather,
hobbies, pets, invitations). v2 adds paraphrase robustness (many phrasings
per question), compound turns (greeting plus question), asking back, a name,
and one polite fallback outside its world.

**3. Alternatives considered.**
- DailyDialog (real dialogue)
- DailyDialog + EmpatheticDialogues (the chat7a lane)
- TinyStories-Instruct (the chat7b lane)
- TinyChat v1 itself (rejected for v2)

**4. Reasons for rejection.**
- DailyDialog: at 13M parameters it "learns the register but not the
  answers" (`CHAT_EXPERIMENTS.md`).
- DailyDialog + EmpatheticDialogues: topic-following, "QA semantics absent"
  (chat7a, loss ~4.1).
- TinyStories-Instruct: fluent narrative with "zero conditioning" at 0.2
  epochs (chat7b).
- TinyChat v1: one or two phrasings per question, so a model answers "what
  is your hobby ?" and fails "what do you enjoy doing ?" (`tinychat_v2.py`
  docstring).

**5. Contamination risk.** None against public benchmarks: the corpus is
generated and no public benchmark is evaluated. The open risk is internal:
the trainer's held-out tail is drawn from the same generator. The tail
measures memorisation of a closed world, not generalisation beyond it.
Exact-duplicate rates are 0.53% (v1_1500), 0% (v2_1500) and 0.22%
(v2_4000), from `corpus/cards/*.stats.json`.

**6. Licence.** Generated by this repository's code; same licence as the
repository. Redistribution allowed.

**7. Measured baseline the next corpus must beat.** None recorded in this
repository. The v1 result in `transformer_cpp` ("the right kind of reply to
every test question under greedy decoding") is qualitative. The baseline is
set by the C1 pilot as answer accuracy on a frozen probe set
(`corpus/eval/tinychat_probe_v1`, Phase E) and bits per byte on the
TinyChat panel member.
