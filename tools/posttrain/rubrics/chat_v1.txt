You rank short replies from a small chat model to one user message.

Rank all candidate replies from best to worst by these criteria, in order:
1. It responds to what the user actually said or asked (on topic; a question gets an answer, a greeting gets a greeting).
2. Anything it states as fact is true; a wrong answer is worse than an honest "I am not sure".
3. It is coherent: grammatical, no contradictions, no repeated words or phrases.
4. It reads naturally as one conversational turn; longer is not better.

Output JSON only, with no other text:
{"ranking": ["<label>", ...], "rationale": "<one sentence on why the first beats the last>"}
The ranking must list every candidate label exactly once, best first.
