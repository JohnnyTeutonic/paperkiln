# Adversarial design review of tools/lab

Reviewer: an Explore agent running Claude Opus 5.5, 7 Nov 2026, one pass, given the code, the brief and the section 9 failure-mode list, and none of the author's conclusions. The same kind of model as the author, so not an independent judge in the sense a person or another maker's model would be. Its text follows, with the absolute path prefix shortened to `tools/lab/`. Answers to every finding are in `review_answers_2026-11-07.md`. Line numbers refer to the reviewed copy, not to the code as corrected.

**How this was checked (reviewer):** read every `.py` file and `examples/` in full. Runtime claims marked "Ran" were confirmed with in-memory `python -B` calls; nothing was written, and the test suite was not run. The frozen modules were not opened, as instructed. `README.md` was not in the review copy.

## Blockers

**1. The verdict is not bound to the ledger or the registration.** `verdict.py:56`. `adaptive_verdict` takes hand-composed dicts, and no CLI command calls it. `rule_sha` is computed but never compared with `frozen_rule_sha`. Guard names are never compared with the registration, so one guard passing is enough. `integrity_problems` defaults to empty, and `exposure.ok` is a caller claim. `v_registration` lets a second registration in, and only `supersedes` is policed. A second plain `outcome` record is accepted, and `verdict` is a free string. Nothing limits re-running a VOID pilot under a new `experiment_id`. Ran: one guard of four, one template group and no dev-read counts gave ADAPTIVE_BETTER with interval [5.0, 5.0]. With `min_seed_pairs=1`, one pair and one group also gave ADAPTIVE_BETTER, despite the docstring at line 4. Fix: a `lab.py verdict` command that takes only a ledger and a rule; recompute the audit from leg records; require sha(rule) to equal `frozen_rule_sha` and guards to equal the registered list; floor `min_seed_pairs` at 2 and set a minimum group count; refuse a second registration or outcome per experiment; count attempts across experiment ids sharing a hypothesis.

**2. The "95%" interval leaves out seed variance, the largest noise source.** `verdict.py:34`. `paired_boot` resamples groups after averaging over seed pairs, so a model-level shift counts as signal. The sign check at line 90 passes one time in four under the null. Ran, under the null, two pairs, 30 groups, practical 7: seed SD 0 gave 0% false ADAPTIVE_BETTER and 0% FIXED_BETTER; seed SD 6.9 points gave 8.1% and 9.2%. The 6.9 comes from the snapshot's `told_of_108_null_sd` of 7.45 items, on the told-recall scale, not T_E. The simulation can't see this: `sim.run_arm` (line 96) has no noise on the evaluation curve. Fix: add seed variance measured from the fixed pair to the interval, or use a seed-level test; two pairs have too few degrees of freedom for a claim, so SCREEN_PROMISING should be the ceiling until more pairs exist; add seed noise to the simulator.

**3. A guard firing on the adaptive arm voids the comparison instead of counting against adaptive.** `controller.py:223`. REVIEW_GUARD has no plan, so the arm halts, the legs differ, and `audit_arms` returns VOID with "Nothing is read". This is the outcome adaptive harm should produce, and it leaves no verdict against adaptive. The state is permanent: `best` keeps its old minimum, no record kind clears a review, and `supersedes` is never read by any consumer. The same applies to a failed leg: `gate_stops` stays at REVIEW_INPUT even after a superseding leg. `sim.run_arm` ignores `action` and trains on regardless; 25 of 240 decisions in `noisy_read` were REVIEW_GUARD, and the harness never showed it. Fix: a `review` record kind with a registered resume-at-balanced rule; run both arms to the end; an adaptive-only guard trip becomes a GUARD_FAILED outcome against adaptive; the simulator honours actions; honour `supersedes` or remove it.

**4. The exposure tilt rewrites the allocation, and neither the tilt nor the plan is recorded.** `exposure.py:29`. Matching tokens per draw on `mean_supervised` overrides the controller. Ran on the synthetic demo pool: correction_toy 2.0 to 1.19, recall_toy 0.8 to 1.12 (reversed), smalltalk_toy 0.5 to 0.99 (cut cancelled), role_toy 0.85 to 0.72. The pilot would be testing mostly a row-length rule. A KL-nearest projection is little better, so the problem is structural: with equal steps and equal tokens, free token shares are over-constrained. `tilt_to_target` also drops `pinned`. `plan_leg` output never reaches the ledger, and `v_leg` has no `decision_sha`. Fix: absorb the token gap by choosing row lengths within a family or within replay, not by moving family shares; refuse a plan where a pushed family loses more than a registered fraction of its push or flips sign; put the plan on the ledger and require `plan_sha` on each leg.

**5. The exposure audit will mostly VOID real comparisons.** `exposure.py:139`. `plan_leg` targets supervised tokens only, but `sampled_decision` is a voiding check at 2%. Ran: a plan matched supervised tokens to 0.03% and still sat 6.45% off on decision tokens. The 0.5% supervised tolerance is below sampling noise: on S52's family mix, between-family variance only, one arm's 32,768 draws have a 0.67% CV, the gap between arms an SD of 0.94%, so P(gap over 0.5%) is about 0.60. No code does the pre-launch MT19937 recount that `correction_loop_pilot.md` requires. Fixed cannot win a comparison that voids, and each void invites a rerun. Fix: `plan_leg` constrains both token totals; a pre-launch realised recount with a deterministic packet adjustment, recorded; tolerances from measured noise.

**6. The firewall checks labels, and its controls are not wired in.** `firewall.py:72`. Role and `instrument_id` are self-declared, and `is_frozen_instrument` is a prefix denylist. Ran: `dev_chatflow_v001`, `" chatflow_v001"`, `analytic-rubric`, `rubric_v2`, `sealed_templates`, `held_T_E` and `T_C_candidate_pool` all pass. `set_sha` is never compared with the splits or a frozen hash, so T_E under a new name passes. `controller_view` skips other experiments before checking role, so a sealed read logged elsewhere returned `[]` and no breach. `check_looks` is called by no production code; `max_dev_reads` is validated and never enforced; `check_shortcut_read` and `prefix_stable` have no production caller; `check_splits` runs only in `firewall-check`. Fix: allowlist one `(instrument_id, set_sha, metric)` per registered experiment; scan all records for breach; call the look cap from `decide`; add T_E and sealed hashes to a deny list by hash.

**7. Controller configuration is unfrozen, and `allow_synthetic` turns off the guards.** `controller.py:97`. `allow_synthetic: true` lifts the specialist-floor check, the priority refusal and the registered-evidence check. The registration has no `config_sha`, so eta, z, floors, caps and policies are never frozen. `arm_policies` is not compared with the registration's arms, and no arm has to be fixed. Ran: `decide` returned ALLOCATE with no registration on the ledger. `floor_source` is a bare string. "registered" is only a label on each record. Fix: `config_sha` in the registration, required in `decide`; refuse `allow_synthetic` when the evidence is registered; require a registration record first, with arm policies equal to the registered ones; give floors provenance fields.

**8. The stage gate reuses the selected fixed pair as control.** `examples/pilot_draft.json:23`. The adaptive pair runs only when the fixed pair read badly, and is then compared with those same models: regression to the mean in adaptive's favour, and the arms are not concurrent. As a bound: if the gate read tracked T_E perfectly and kept the worst 27% of fixed draws, the bias is about +6 points against a 7-point margin. `practical_rule` sets its value after the fixed pair, so a frozen rule hash cannot exist at registration. `development_instrument` says the metric is "chosen by the signal read", a forking path. Fix: gate on a separate fixed seed excluded from the comparison, and run fixed and adaptive pairs together afterwards; freeze the formula and record its evaluated value as a separate pre-adaptive record; freeze the metric before the signal read.

## Majors

**9. "Noise earns no weight" is false.** `controller.py:239`. Dividing by mean conservative excess makes the push scale-free. Ran: a conservative excess of 0.001 nats, with the others at zero, reached the full 2.0. With all families exactly at their floor, a family moved in 59% of decisions at z=1 and 11% at z=2, nearly always to the cap. Design B's 0.15-nat bar is absent from the controller. Fix: normalise by a fixed scale such as 0.15 nats, require a minimum absolute excess, freeze z at 2 or higher with a family-count correction.

**10. Multiplier state is stale and can be compounded.** `controller.py:114`. `decisions[-1]` is whichever decision came last, not the decision for leg-1. Ran: recording a dry run twice for the same leg moved correction_toy from 1.80 to 2.00, and `audit-decisions` would pass. `v_decision` does not require `next_leg`. Compounding uses pre-tilt multipliers, not realised dose. Fix: one decision per (arm, leg), state only from the decision for leg-1, compound from realised shares.

**11. A development read is not tied to its leg.** `controller.py:108`. Ran: a read for leg 3, with a foreign checkpoint, another `set_sha`, metric "points" and a different instrument, drove a decision for leg 2. `dev_read.checkpoint` is never compared with `checkpoint_out`; `checkpoint_in` is never chained; the registered `start_checkpoint` is never compared with leg 0; `reads[-1]` is the highest leg, not leg-1; gaps and duplicates misalign the reversed zip at line 140. Fix: a read of exactly leg-1 from that leg's `checkpoint_out`, with a constant instrument, set and metric, and a contiguous chain from the registered start.

**12. An exposure key that doesn't match a family silently disables stalling.** `controller.py:121`. Dose reads as 0 when the keys differ, so the family is pushed forever. Packet keys such as `stance:correction` sit beside held-out `contrast:correction`, and `adapters.to_exposure` passes packet keys through. Ran: two identical families under a key mismatch ended as `pushed 1.5` and `stalled 0.5`. Human and base rows have `sampled_decision` of 0, so they can never stall. Fix: a registered mapping from packet family to controller family, fail closed on any uncovered family, supervised dose where decision tokens are not defined.

**13. Allocation and dose use different units.** `exposure.py:3`. Shares are supervised tokens, while stall dose and design B's dose are decision tokens. On S52's leg, decision share runs from 18% (correction) to 64% (passage), so a "balanced" fixed arm is about 3.5 times unbalanced in the unit that matters. T_M has 7 families and T_C 5, and no mapping exists. Fix: allocate in decision tokens, with supervised tokens as the matching constraint, and freeze the family mapping.

**14. The row checker is not independent of the generator, and one check is wrong.** `rowcheck.py:68`. The target `m["value"]` comes from the generator's own metas, so a state-machine error passes. Ran: a stale gold ("Paris" after "Sorry, I moved to Rome") gave no defects. Ran: a value present only in the question also gave no defects, because `seen` is updated before the check. `VALUE_OPS` omits `like_recall`, both names and own preference; a meta with no value skips silently; attribution is not checked; `check_rows` reports no count of checks applied; it runs only from `integrate.py`. Fix: derive the expected value from the text with a separately written tracker covering corrections and speakers; check `seen` before appending the current turn; add the missing operations; report coverage per check.

**15. `error_verdict` breaks the module's stated rules.** `verdict.py:130`. Ran: one seed pair gave `VERIFIED_ROWS_ONLY` with `operating_choice` verified. A practical margin of -50 was accepted and gave SELECTION_ADDS. No positive-margin check, no sign check, no minimum pairs on this branch. SELECTION_ADDS skips the chatflow bar that `correction_loop_pilot.md` rule 3 requires. It has no three-arm exposure audit. Fix: the same checks as `adaptive_verdict` on every claim branch, and the chatflow bar.

**16. Failure selection is not clipped as promised.** `miner.py:69`. Renormalising after the clip breaks the 0.25 to 3 bound. Ran: the hot cell reached 5.71 times V's share. The mean rate covers all cells, not the verified mix; `rate[k]` raises KeyError for n=0; `reproducible` is unused. Fix: solve a capped simplex, and implement the registered rule.

**17. The miner's firewall is a string argument.** `miner.py:19`. `label_clean` checks only the `split` value the caller passes, and never reads row provenance, so T_E rows labelled "T_M" pass. `tally` is an unguarded second door, and `target_verified=True` is hard-coded. Nothing limits mining to one round. Fix: check each row's provenance part inside `tally`, and take a registered round number that refuses a second round.

**18. `taxonomy.counts` pools model-induced histories with clean ones.** `taxonomy.py:92`. Ran: one clean and one model-induced event gave a single row with n=2. `tally` drops a failure silently when its label maps to `None`, and `FROZEN_LABELS` is unused. Fix: add history to the key, and raise on a failure with no category.

**19. The authorisation gate honours agent-written and synthetic grants.** `gate.py:41`. Ran: a grant with evidence `synthetic`, producer `Claude Opus 5.5` and zero-cost action fields returned `permit: True`. `review: passed` is written into the registration record by its own author, with no reviewer named. `regs[-1]` means a later registration under the same id inherits the grant. `action.l4_minutes` is not compared with `budget.max_l4_minutes`, and `spec_sha` matches nothing on the ledger. `check` does not consume the grant. `consumption` is not validated against the grant's `action_sha`. `--today` is caller-supplied, and there is no revocation. Fix: a grant with registered evidence and a named non-agent producer; review as a separate record by a named reviewer; bind grants to the registration's hash and compare budgets; permit and consumption as one locked append; an out-of-band signature is the only real boundary here.

**20. The ledger has no external anchor, and one character bricks it.** `ledger.py:233`. The chain is unkeyed, and `do_dry_run` loads without `expected_head`. A full rewrite passes. Tail truncation passes too, which erases a failed leg or a bad read and clears REVIEW_INPUT. Line 243 uses `splitlines()`, and `canon` keeps U+2028 and U+0085 raw; ran: one record became 2 lines, so a pasted authorisation quote would make every later `load` fail. Fix: commit the head outside the ledger after each append and require it on every run; split on newline only.

**21. Overhead is tolerated and hidden.** `verdict.py:71`. The central hypothesis is competence per unit of compute, yet nothing tests it; `trajectory` decides nothing. Matched fixed carries the same legs and restarts, so controller cost is invisible; a real fixed curriculum would not pay it. `TRANSITION_PER_PAIR` is a plug that closes the 2.7-hour figure, and no boundary has been timed. `cpu_minutes`, mining and session overhead are outside `spent_l4`. `fixed_minutes` is a caller number. Fix: compute minutes from ledger records, CPU included at a registered rate; give the fixed arm the steps saved by not having boundaries, or add a third single-run reference; time a boundary before registration; make a per-compute endpoint part of the rule.

**22. Forgetting is largely unobserved.** `controller.py:145`. `_replay` has no read and no guard, though zero-dose falls of 0.14 to 0.93 nats are on record. No read of the start checkpoint exists. `rise_beyond_noise` ignores the SE of `best`, and `best` is a biased minimum. The 0.15 limit plus noise sits around S51's contrast losses of 0.06 to 0.22, so most of the recorded trade would pass. `pilot_draft.json` makes mean task points primary and moves design B's worst-family bar to "reported apart". `replay_floor_held` has no caller. Fix: read replay families and the start checkpoint in both arms; restore design B's bar as a non-compensatory rule; add per-family T_E guards.

**23. Proposer rankings are not comparable and are not learned.** `proposer.py:45`. `rank` scores told recall, T_E points, J1, LAMBADA and two bases on one "points" scale. Author-set priors decide the order, and prior SD counts twice. A measurement with mean 0 gets E[gain] 2.79. Ran: with `sft_saturated` true, `more_pretraining` is 11th of 12, and `fixed_balanced_stronger` stays top: the rule meant to promote pretraining does not. It never reads the ledger. `facts_2026-11-07.json` sets `fixed_baseline_on_pk_sft_v1: true`, while `costs.py` says "partial". Fix: rank only within one base and one endpoint, exclude measurements from gain, update priors from outcome records or say plainly it is an opinion list, correct the fact to false.

**24. Decisions recompute, but allocations don't.** `lab.py:54`. Config sits off the ledger, as a hash only. `pool` is neither stored nor hashed, and the plan is discarded. `verify` never recomputes `decision_sha`, and `cmd_audit` depends on a `next_leg` that `v_decision` does not require. `--evidence` defaults to `registered`. Fix: ledger the config, the pool hash and the plan; recompute `decision_sha` in `validate`; default evidence to refuse.

**25. Exposure audit holes in the other direction.** `exposure.py:116`. Ran: legs numbered 0, 5, 9 against 0, 1, 2 passed; a leg at 1.5 times dose offset by one at 0.5 times passed. Totals are cumulative and the zip does not check leg numbers. The start checkpoint is compared only for leg 0, so `sim.one_comparison`'s +10 offset skips the second pair. lr, warm-up, optimiser, batch, `source_commit` and generator are not compared. LR-weighted and distinct-scenario gaps are warnings, so a repetition-heavy adaptive arm can still win. The expected-distinct-rows formula is wrong: 500.0 reported, 467.4 true. `capacity_binding` is only a flag. Fix: audit per leg and per pair with leg equality, compare the recipe fields, make scenario repetition a voiding check above a limit, use `rows_total` in the formula, fail closed on capacity.

**26. The simulator cannot fail in the ways that matter.** `sim.py:73`. It never calls `plan_leg`. Decision tokens are a constant fraction and total dose is fixed, so `audit_arms` is vacuous there. It reads the arm 4 times where the draft says 3. No seed noise on evaluation, ignores actions, models no tilt. Conclusions drawn from it, such as the priority formula losing, reflect the world as built. Fix: run the full path of decide, plan, realised recount, audit and verdict, with seed noise, row-length variance and action handling.

**27. Many tests don't test what their names say.** `test_lab.py`. `test_checker_imports_no_generator`: `^` without MULTILINE cannot fail. `test_no_sealed_ids_are_committed`: searches for a literal string. `test_no_collapse_on_noise`: asserts caps that hold by construction. `test_a_null_world_rarely_claims_adaptive`: no seed noise, allows 10% false claims. `test_saturation_lets_pretraining_overtake_sft`: never compares ranks. `test_mining_outside_t_m_is_refused`: a string argument only. `test_selection_clip_and_reproducibility`: never checks the clip. `test_counts_keep_tiers_apart`: history not covered. `test_overhead_counts`: a 20% case only. `test_no_output_language_claims_recursion`: scans `MEANING` only. `test_outcome_cannot_be_superseded`: the flag only. `test_validation_read_stops_the_controller`: same arm only. `test_plan_meets_the_fixed_arms_tokens`: ignores decision tokens. `test_hard_family_gets_no_extra_dose`: one seed, last leg. `test_no_shell_call`: sees only attribute calls. Fix: rewrite each to assert the property its name states.

## Minors

**28. `firewall-check` is a membership oracle and takes its pin from argv.** `lab.py:141`. An arbitrary `--used` list returns a hit count, which allows bisection on sealed group ids. `--pin` and `--expect` let a decoy file pass. Fix: hard-code the pin and count, report only pass or breach, limited to ledgered set hashes.

**29. Stop rules and arm symmetry.** `controller.py:229`. `matched_pilot` is not forced when a fixed arm exists, so STOP_SATURATED ends only adaptive. `spent_l4` is per arm. The saturated fallback is flagged only in reason text. An adaptive arm that never moved reads as NO_ADAPTIVE_BENEFIT, not "not exercised". `priority_decide` has no guard. Fix: require `matched_pilot` for any two-arm experiment, budget per experiment, a structured flag and a distinct verdict.

**30. Parameter ranges are not enforced.** `controller.py:54`. `load_config` accepted patience 0, z 0, `multiplier_max` 1000, `multiplier_min` 0.001, replay 0 and `min_gain_nats` 0. The condition that `min_gain_nats` exceed z times the SE of a difference is unchecked. With 4 legs and 3 reads, patience 2 can fire only at the last decision. A stalled family at minimum share can fall below `min_dose_tokens`, which clears the stall. `gate_stops` checks only the latest read for missing families. Fix: enforce these bounds at load, and check every read.

**31. Smaller defects.** `history.py:81`. `replay` uses a gain SE as a read SE. `exposure.plan_leg` computes draws per row over `rows_total`, not `val_start`. `proposer` lists adaptive entries on different cost bases.

## Could anything start a GPU job, spend money or read sealed data?

GPU and spend: no such path in the lab's own code, whose imports are standard library only, apart from numpy through frozen modules. Unverified: `adapters.load_frozen` runs `exec` on pinned frozen modules, outside the scan and outside the reviewer's read scope. Sealed data: `integrate.check_sealed` and `firewall-check` read the sealed file, and output counts only, subject to finding 28.

## The ten failure modes, as reviewed

1. Adaptive overfitting: partly defended; self-declared labels, an unwired look cap and a metric picked after reading leave it open.
2. Noisy marginal gain: gain cannot raise a weight, which holds; excess noise reaches the cap in 59% of at-floor decisions at z=1.
3. Curriculum collapse: caps hold in range, but are unfrozen, and tilt moves families by row length.
4. Historical selection: banners and the evidence filter keep history out of decisions; the label is self-declared, the stage gate adds new selection.
5. Hidden exposure: detection exists but is cumulative and misses recipe fields; tilt and unrecorded plans hide the real change.
6. Generator/verifier collusion: not defended.
7. Loss of learned behaviours: partly defended; replay unread, no start read, a firing guard voids, design B's bar dropped.
8. Controller overhead: not tested.
9. False claims of recursive improvement: verdict wording scoped, but only tested against `MEANING`.
10. Cross-model confounds: `base_id` checks run inside one experiment, but it is a free string, and the proposer ranks across bases.
