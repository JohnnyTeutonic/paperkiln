# Tiny-chat programme: interim results (25 Oct 2026, 20 of 66 post jobs home)

Descriptive only. No registered test is run here: E5's contrasts, E1's depth slope, the recursion and MoR
comparisons and HL0 on the winners' picks are computed when each arm is complete, by `analyze.py`. Cells
hold 1-3 seeds of an eventual 3; differences of 3-4 points between cells are inside seed noise at this n.

## The benchmark in one paragraph

Carry-5: a fact is planted early in a five-turn dialogue and queried later; each item has a single-turn
twin (fact and question in one turn). Y = forced-choice accuracy on the multi-turn item, with the model's
own earlier replies as history, minus accuracy on its twin. Y = 0 means spreading the exchange over turns
costs nothing. The validity gate needs twin accuracy at least chance + 10 points (chance 0.25).

## What the 20 runs show (descriptive)

1. **Every run passes the validity gate** (twin 0.51-0.58 against chance 0.25): the models answer.
2. **No run has Y >= 0.** Y lies between -0.025 and -0.097, mean -0.060.
3. **The cost is the carry-over itself, not error propagation.** Mean Y with gold history is -0.052 against
   -0.060 with self history: the model's own replies cost 0.008.
4. **Within Carry-5 the cost does not grow with distance.** Gold-history accuracy at distances 1-4 is 0.559,
   0.508, 0.584, 0.625: a flat cost of the multi-turn format. (Carry-12's Stage L0 does show a distance cost:
   pick_e1_L32 -0.061 SUPPORTED, pick_e1_L16 -0.030 SIGNIFICANT_BELOW_SESOI.)
5. **Dialogue in pretraining lowers dialogue loss without improving Y.** Dialogue panel loss falls from 5.16
   (s0) to 3.15 (s70); mean Y by cell is s0 -0.037, s17 -0.073, s35 -0.078, s70 -0.043.
6. **Depth does not help at 20M so far**: L4 -0.057 (n 1), L16 -0.044, L32 -0.079. L2 and most of L4 are to come.
7. **Size may help**: 60M L32 -0.047 against 20M L32 -0.079, with the lowest dialogue loss (3.14); n = 2.
8. **Recursion and MoR show no advantage so far** (n = 1 each): R4 -0.097, MC7 -0.065, MoR3 -0.063.
9. **SFT does not remove the cost**: the pretrained models' Y is of the same size.
10. **Families**: correction is the hardest (gold 0.353, twin 0.386); instruction is near ceiling (0.978).
