# Learned cross-layer connections for long-gap carry-over: literature check (24 Oct 2026)

Question: does a mechanism that learns, per token, which earlier activations to strengthen (beyond the fixed residual sum) improve multi-turn carry-over at long gaps in 5M-150M decoders? Every claim was read from the arXiv abstract, HTML or PDF, or the official GitHub page, on 24 Oct 2026. Context: `../tiny_chat_litcheck_2026-10-23.md`, `../tiny_chat_2026-10/RECURSION_LITCHECK.md`.

## 2. paperkiln's attention-residual family

- `nn::AttnResStack` (`include/microtorch/nn.hpp`, `src/nn.cpp`, anchor `python/attn_res_reference.py`) is Kimi's AttnRes (2603.15031; the code cites it through Kimi K3, 2607.24653): zero-init pseudo-queries, parameter-free RMSNorm on keys, raw values, softmax over depth; full form (`block_size` 0) and block form. mtstudio's `AttnResLM` uses block size 2 (attention + FFN summed into one bank entry per transformer block, two queries per block plus the output query). **Same mechanism, same parameterisation.**
- **DenseFormer is a relative, not a special case.** Its weights are unconstrained static reals initialised to the identity, over residual-stream states. AttnRes weights are convex and content-dependent. Kimi's "input-independent mixing" ablation is the DenseFormer-like point.
- **MUDDFormer is different.** It generates weights per position from the current hidden state (query side, an MLP, no softmax, keys input-independent) and feeds Q, K, V and the residual from four separate mixtures. paperkiln's family is single-stream with a static query: roughly MUDD's residual stream alone, with another weight function. Multiway needs an attention sublayer that takes separate Q, K and V inputs, a new block wiring in the IR (the ops exist; this is an inference from `ir.hpp`, not tested).
- **Gate status in ember** (LOCAL_LLM_STACK.md section 4): `list`, `append`, `depth_attend` are IR on ember CPU, CUDA and WASM; attnres tiny 40/40 (ec53c26; device ops 1232431).
- **The llama port:** `llama_attnres` (paperkiln c48ca962; keramos family `llama_attnres`) puts the same mechanism on the E1 base (RMSNorm, RoPE, SwiGLU, tied) through `arch.custom.attn_res` = {"form": "full", "scale": "sources"}, with a fused `ops::depth_attend` on host and device and an IR of existing ops (`list`, `append`, `depth_attend`, `affine`). The `attnres` family itself stays GPT-2 blocks (LayerNorm, learned positions, GELU). Ember gated `llama_attnres` on CPU, CUDA and WASM (model ar3).
- Under pre-norm, a uniform average of the sources is the plain residual sum scaled by 1/S, so the normalised sublayer input is identical up to eps: zero-init AttnRes starts as the plain model.

## 3. Open vs taken (conservative)

**Taken.** Static, gated and token-dependent cross-layer mixing (everything above), including per-stream mixing (MUDD, DCA, RD-AttnRes) and per-token depth selection. Perplexity results down to 75M-124M (DCA, ResFormer, MHAR, RD-AttnRes with 5 seeds, modded-nanogpt). Loops combined with cross-layer connections (Hyperloop, MeSH, Depth-Attention). In-context probes at 1B (LIMe KV Maps and Induction) and IOI at 8M and 1B (Burns).

**Open (none found).** Any cross-layer mechanism measured on multi-turn carry-over stratified by gap (1 vs 6-13 turns) and interference, at 5M-150M, after SFT, multi-seed, with near and twin controls. Also open: token-level depth-weight analysis at the retrieval position (Kimi averages weights over tokens). The contribution would be the measurement and the scale, not the mechanism. Searches covered arXiv and the web; the October 2026 stream of AttnRes follow-ups means a very recent paper could be missed.

**What the literature predicts.** General loss gains (larger at small width per DCA; needing depth per DenseFormer, HC and Kimi); a weak hint that the value route carries retrieval (MUDD's V stream, Burns' IOI, LIMe's KV Maps); no source for a gap-dependent effect. Static mixing is null or harmful twice (Kimi's DenseFormer row; LIMe's induction row).

## 4. One arm: design

- **Mechanism.** Full AttnRes at sublayer granularity (embedding plus every attention and FFN output, 2L+1 sources: AttnResStack's `block_size` 0 with the sublayers as its layers, which `block_size` 1 equals exactly; `llama_attnres` has no `block_size` and names this `form` "full"), zero-init static queries, on the llama base. Reasons: the best-ablated content-dependent variant; parameter-trivial; already gated in ember; and it is Jonathan's idea in its cleanest form, since tokens choose which earlier activations to strengthen. Not the input-dependent query: d x d per sublayer is ~3.3M parameters at 20m_L16 and breaks matching. Not MUDD first: it needs four-stream wiring and has no published comparison against AttnRes.
- **Shape and matching.** 20m_L16 (d 320, 33 sources, +10.6K parameters = 0.05%). The control is the plain cell at the same shape (E1's 20m_L16 if the long-carry base reuses it), paired by seed (1-3), with identical tokens, data order and SFT. Use the plain cell's learning rate, as Kimi did (conservative against the arm). Report tokens/s: paperkiln composes depth attention from tape ops, and the unfused AttnRes in 2607.27230 ran at 0.2-0.5x.
- **Registered prediction (Jonathan's hypothesis, H-gap).** Define the gap interaction D = [Y_gold,long(AttnRes) - Y_gold,long(plain)] - [Y_near(AttnRes) - Y_near(plain)], with long = recall items at distance >= 6 and near = Carry-12's near rendering (distance 1). Prediction: D > SESOI, and the benefit's slope on distance (correction family, 1-11) is positive.
- **Counter-hypothesis (literature-led, registered as the default).** The gain is a general LM improvement. D lies within SESOI, and at matched FineWeb panel loss the arm sits on the plain cells' loss-to-Y line (the isoplot test from RECURSION_LITCHECK). Second counter: no main effect at 20M (Kimi's DenseFormer row, registry 0001, HC at n = 1).
- **Secondary, descriptive.** Benefit by interference count (0-3). Depth weights at the answer token vs the mean, focusing on the embedding and early sources: the direct test of "selective strengthening". Routing entropy per layer (collapse check).
- **Step 2, conditional on a main effect.** Add a separate value route (RD-AttnRes, one vector per layer, the cheapest multiway step). Then a recursion x AttnRes cell, with loop applications as sources (Depth-Attention, Hyperloop).

## 5. Failure modes

- No gain from static or near-static mixing (DenseFormer at 436M; registry 0001; HC at n = 1). Gains grow with depth, so a shallow cell can fail for structural reasons.
- Routing collapses to near-uniform in deep layers (2605.18855). The model then behaves as the plain model (section 2), which shows as a null.
- Init: zero queries are required (Kimi; MUDD calls identity init critical). Deep-narrow needed extra normalisation in MUDD (PrePostDANorm).
- Gains shrink with long training (Hyperloop ties at 100B tokens; HC and MUDD ViT). At the programme's tokens per parameter, a gain may be faster convergence rather than capacity.
- Cost: activation memory +17-29% (MUDD, HC); report Y per L4-hour.
- Multi-head depth routing is unsettled (Kimi H = 16 worse; MHAR H = 4-8 better). Keep H = 1.
- Confound: lower loss lifts every Y; only D and the isoplot separate carry-over from general quality.
